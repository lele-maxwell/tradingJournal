# Trading Journal App — Product Overview

# Product Vision

This application is a structured trading discipline and performance journal designed to help traders improve consistency, emotional control, strategy execution, and trade review processes.

The app is not a broker, charting platform, or signal provider.

Its purpose is to help traders:
- validate setups before entering trades
- document trades properly
- track psychology and discipline
- upload screenshots for review
- analyze weekly and monthly performance

The app should encourage:
- patience
- consistency
- emotional awareness
- strategic discipline
- accountability

---

# Core Philosophy

The application revolves around one major question:

> “Did the trader follow their strategy correctly?”

Every feature inside the app should support:
- execution quality
- discipline tracking
- behavioral improvement
- review and self-analysis

---

# Main Features

# 1. Strategy Checklist System

Before entering a trade, the user validates whether their strategy conditions were respected.

This section uses checkboxes/toggles.

## Example Checklist Conditions

### Market Structure
- Support respected
- Resistance respected
- Trendline respected
- Order block respected
- Retest confirmed
- Rejection candle formed
- Liquidity sweep occurred
- Market structure shift confirmed
- Higher timeframe aligned

### Session Conditions
- London session
- New York session
- Avoided low-volume hours

### Execution Conditions
- Entry confirmation respected
- Risk management respected
- No impulsive entry

---

# Checklist Scoring

The app should automatically calculate setup quality.

Example:

8 / 10 confirmations met

This score helps traders identify:
- high-quality setups
- weak setups
- profitable patterns
- strategy consistency

---

# 2. Trade Journal System

The journal stores the actual trade execution information.

## Trade Information

### General
- Asset / Pair
- Buy or Sell
- Strategy name

### Entry & Exit
- Entry price
- Exit price
- Stop Loss (SL)
- Take Profit (TP)

### Risk Management
- Position size
- Risk %
- Risk-to-Reward ratio (RR)

### Time Information
- Entry time
- Exit time
- Trade duration
- Entry timeframe
- Higher timeframe analysis

---

# Self-Assessment Questions

- Did I follow my plan?
- Did I move SL emotionally?
- Did I enter too early?
- Did I overtrade today?

---

# 4. Screenshot System

The trader should be able to upload multiple screenshots for every trade.

## Screenshot Types

### Before Entry
Chart before entering the trade.

### After Exit
Trade result and outcome.

### Higher Timeframe Analysis
Larger market structure analysis.

---

# Screenshot Goals

Screenshots help traders:
- visually review setups
- identify repeated mistakes
- compare winning vs losing trades
- improve pattern recognition
